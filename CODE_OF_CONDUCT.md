# Community Code of Conduct

This project welcomes people of every background and experience level. These expectations apply in issues, pull requests, discussions and other spaces maintained for this project.

- Be respectful. Discuss the work and evidence; avoid personal attacks, harassment, threats and discriminatory language.
- Help newcomers. Ask for missing context kindly and explain assumptions.
- Respect privacy. Do not share another person’s identity, credentials, private messages or personal information without permission.
- Keep participation relevant. No spam, unsolicited promotion or coordinated manipulation of stars, votes or reviews.
- Credit other people’s work, and disclose your connection when sharing a project.

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content, close disruptive threads, or restrict participation. Responses should fit the severity and repetition of the behavior, with an explanation when appropriate and safe.

To report abuse privately, use GitHub’s **Report content** menu on the affected content or [GitHub’s abuse reporting route](https://support.github.com/contact/report-abuse). Do not post private evidence in an issue. For a technical vulnerability, follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
